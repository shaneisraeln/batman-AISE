The Threat Surface

Traditional WAFs Are Blind to Model Theft

Standard API gateways inspect single requests in isolation: malformed JSON, SQLi strings, or raw IP floods. They cannot detect an attacker sending valid feature vectors that systematically map your model's decision boundaries.

⚡

Model Extraction & Theft

Attackers submit high-entropy query sequences to reverse-engineer proprietary model weights, hyperplanes, or training data distributions.

🎯

Decision Boundary Probing

Fine-grained iterative perturbation queries designed to discover adversarial vulnerabilities and bypass classification thresholds.

⚠️

Distributional Shift & Anomalies

Extreme norm deviations and out-of-distribution inputs that degrade model reliability or trigger high-cost edge cases.

Engineering Integrity

Scope & Honest Limitations

Security is about reducing risk, not peddling magic. BATMAN is a behavioral inference security layer — here is our honest threat boundary.

  • It does not claim to stop every cyberattack across your perimeter.
  • It does not guarantee zero false positives — detection thresholds must be calibrated against your actual workload.
  • It does not replace your infrastructure WAF, IAM access controls, or network-level firewalls.
  • It evaluates sequences and behavioral patterns; single-query subtle evasion requires model-level robustness defenses.
